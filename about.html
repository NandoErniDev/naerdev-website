I’m Nando. First year of an Informatiker EFZ apprenticeship in application development, and learning to build for iPhone.

Informatiker EFZ Application development Lucerne, CH 47.0502° N / 8.3093° E
Nando ErniLucerne, 2026

I like the moment an idea becomes something that works. Not the grand reveal — the small one, when a screen finally behaves the way you imagined it.

Most of my time goes into learning Swift and SwiftUI properly. I started on a Windows PC, moved to a MacBook Air, and now work from small exercises towards complete apps. I type the code myself rather than copying it.

Outside work I photograph the places I travel to, play saxophone in a youth music ensemble, train in the evenings and follow Apple and technology closely. I want to publish an app of my own, enter the Swift Student Challenge, and one day work on Apple platforms professionally.

This site is built by hand: no framework, no build step, no trackers. It is also a place to keep the travel journal and the projects in one record.

Working notes

A few things I keep coming back to.

Not values on a wall. Just rules that have survived a few projects.

Clarity over cleverness

The best solution is usually the one someone else can understand six months from now.

Details with a reason

Spacing, wording and motion should make something easier to use — never just louder.

Learn by making

I would rather ship a small, honest version and improve it than wait for a perfect idea.

I’m at the beginning. That is the part I like most.

There is still so much to learn, and I have the time.
