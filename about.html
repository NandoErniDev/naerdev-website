About

I’m Nando, an apprentice software developer from the Lucerne region.

I am in the first year of an apprenticeship as an Informatiker EFZ, specialising in application development. What I enjoy most is the programming itself: taking an idea and turning it into something that works.

I take photographs wherever I travel, play the saxophone, train in the evenings and follow Apple and technology closely.
