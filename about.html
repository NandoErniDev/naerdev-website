Nando ErniLucerne, 2026

I’m Nando. I’m in the first year of an Informatiker EFZ apprenticeship in application development, and I’m learning to build for iPhone.

What I enjoy most is the moment an idea becomes something that works. Not the grand reveal — the small one, when a screen finally behaves the way you imagined it.

Outside work I’m learning Swift and SwiftUI so I can build iPhone apps properly. I want to publish an app of my own, enter the Swift Student Challenge, and one day work on Apple platforms professionally.

I take photographs wherever I travel, play the saxophone in a youth music ensemble, train in the evenings and follow Apple and technology closely.

What I care about

Three things I keep coming back to.

Clarity over cleverness

The best solution is usually the one someone else can understand six months from now.

Details with a reason

Spacing, wording and motion should make something easier to use — never just louder.

Learn by making

I would rather ship a small, honest version and improve it than wait for a perfect idea.

I’m at the beginning. That is the part I like most.

There is still so much to learn, and I have the time.
