A note on the pictures

The photographs are arriving slowly.

The routes, places and notes are real. The photographs are being sorted place by place, so some plates are intentionally empty for now. They will fill in without changing the shape of the journal.

No stock photography. No borrowed landscapes. When a place has no picture yet, the page says so.
