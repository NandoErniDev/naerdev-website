Beyond the screen

Music, light
and a little movement.

The things I do when I’m not in Xcode. They are not separate from the work — they are where the patience and the ideas come from.

01 · Jugendmusik

Playing together is a different kind of listening.

I play saxophone in a youth music ensemble. Rehearsals, concerts and the small moments in between have taught me more about timing and attention than any tutorial.

In a group, you cannot simply play your part and hope. You have to hear the whole, leave space, and trust the people beside you. That is a useful thing to remember when building software, too.

The photograph is still being added — the place in the layout is ready for it.

02 · Saxophone

One instrument, a thousand small decisions.

I came to the saxophone through the music I listened to, and stayed for the way it makes an afternoon disappear.

Music is how I focus, train and unwind. It is also the best reminder I know that something can be technically correct and still need feeling.

03 · Photography

Looking for the light.

I photograph the places I travel to. The best of it ends up in the travel journal.

Places, not postcards

I look for the ordinary moment that makes a place feel real: a doorway, a table, the last light on a wall.

Patience, mostly

Good light does not arrive on schedule. Photography is a good excuse to wait and pay attention.

04 · Everyday

Training, Lucerne, and a good coffee.

Training

Evening workouts at home, swimming in summer and short cycling tours with friends. Movement clears the head better than another cup of coffee.

Lucerne

Walking through the old town, past the Kapellbrücke and the KKL, is still one of the best ways to end a day.

On repeat

A soundtrack for focus.

Mostly pop with a little edge: OneRepublic, The Script, Ed Sheeran, Imagine Dragons, Dean Lewis, Alex Warren, Leony and Henry Moodie.

Music helps me concentrate while I code, push through a workout and think things over in quieter moments.
