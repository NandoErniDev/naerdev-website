01 / Jugendmusik

Playing together is a different kind of listening.

I play saxophone in a youth music ensemble. Rehearsals and concerts have taught me more about timing and attention than any tutorial.

In a group you cannot simply play your part and hope. You have to hear the whole, leave space, and trust the people beside you. That is a useful thing to remember when building software, too.

Most of what I know about ensemble playing came from getting it wrong in front of everyone, then trying again the next week.

02 / Saxophone

One instrument, a thousand small decisions.

I came to the saxophone through the music I listened to, and stayed for the way it makes an afternoon disappear.

Music is how I focus, train and unwind. It is also the best reminder I know that something can be technically correct and still need feeling.

03 / Photography

Looking for the light.

I photograph the places I travel to. The best of it ends up in the travel journal.

Places, not postcards

I look for the ordinary moment that makes a place feel real: a doorway, a table, the last light on a wall.

Patience, mostly

Good light does not arrive on schedule. Photography is a good excuse to wait and pay attention.
